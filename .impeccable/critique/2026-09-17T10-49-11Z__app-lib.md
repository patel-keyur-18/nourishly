## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: Not generic. Regional dish names (rotli, thepla, khakhra, bhinda nu shaak, idli/sambar) run through real screen copy, portions are modeled on katori/vaatki household measures rather than grams-first entry, and the recipe-fork feature exists specifically because this household cooks with less oil than the catalog average — a feature no generic calorie app has reason to build. The honesty-over-completeness principle (unknown ≠ zero) is implemented as real report logic, not just copy. It weakens on one point: the screen designed to demonstrate that principle most concretely — the portion/food-detail nutrient preview with its "won't be counted as zero" message — isn't built yet (see P1 below).

**Deterministic scan**: `detect.mjs --json docs/design/prototype.html` (the only detector-legible artifact — Dart isn't scannable) returned exit 2, 55 findings: 2 warnings (`side-tab` decorative accent borders on an info callout and an insight-list item) and 53 advisory (32 font-sizes and 20 radii off the DESIGN.md scale, 1 undocumented color). Most of the advisory volume is explainable noise rather than real drift: device-frame bezel radii, the gallery wrapper's own responsive marketing heading, illustrative chart/icon shapes (water-bottle, glass, bar-chart corners), and a scrim color that already has a documented dark-mode equivalent expressed as a token elsewhere. The two real warnings and the volume of `.5px` micro-typography in dense tables are worth a cleanup pass on the prototype file itself, but don't reach into the shipped app, which the detector can't see at all.

**Visual overlays**: Not available — no browser automation tool was exposed in this session, so no live `[Human]`-tab overlay could be produced. This is reported as a fallback signal per the critique protocol, not a failure.

## Overall Impression

The design system is followed with real discipline — the "never colour alone," "no red for over-target," and flat-at-rest elevation rules all show up correctly in shared components (`StatusChip`, `NutrientBar`, `EnergyRing`), and the regional-food specificity is genuine, not decorative. The biggest gap isn't visual polish, it's completeness and cross-screen truth: one of the 13 approved screens (meal templates) doesn't exist yet, the screen meant to prove the product's core "honesty" claim at the moment of logging is missing its nutrient preview, and two screens quietly disagree about the household's own water target. These read like an app that's further along on tokens and components than on wiring every screen to the same source of truth.

## What's Working

1. **Screen-reader semantics are conclusion-first, not coordinate-first.** `EnergyRing` and `NutrientBar` in `packages/nourishly_ui` wrap themselves in `Semantics(label: …)` with hand-written sentences ("82 g of 95 g target, 86 per cent") — better than most production apps.
2. **The design rules are enforced structurally, not by per-screen discipline.** `StatusChip` only ever exposes violet for over-target, never red, and always pairs a dot with a label — so no future screen can accidentally reintroduce the scolding red the product principles explicitly reject.
3. **The recipe-fork feature is genuinely household-specific and self-documenting** — its own doc comment in `food_portion_screen.dart` explains why forking rather than editing the shared catalog row is the honest choice, verifiable against PRODUCT.md's positioning claims.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Meal templates (approved screen 7) doesn't exist in the shipped app**
- **Why it matters**: PRODUCT.md lists it as in-scope v1.0; decisions.md treats all 13 screens as decided facts. It's the highest-leverage feature for a household eating the same thali/thepla/idli combinations repeatedly, and its absence directly hurts heuristic 7.
- **Fix**: Build screen 7 as approved (cards with contents), or explicitly log its deferral in decisions.md the way the four other departures are recorded — right now there's no way to tell "missed" from "decided."
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable shape` (to scope the build) or a note in decisions.md if it's a deliberate cut.

**[P1] Portion/food-detail screen (screen 6) omits the nutrient breakdown and the unknown-data honesty message**
- **Why it matters**: this is the screen where "honesty over completeness" — the product's core differentiator — was designed to become visible at the moment of logging. Right now `food_portion_screen.dart` shows only a quality-tier chip and a placeholder sentence; a user gets zero nutrient feedback until after they save.
- **Fix**: Port the nutrient-bar block from `MacroCard`/`NutrientBar` into `FoodPortionScreen`, reusing the unknown-nutrient em-dash pattern already implemented in `daily_report_screen.dart`.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden` (closes a real functional gap against the product's own claims).

**[P1] Water target disagrees between the Water screen and the Goals screen**
- **Why it matters**: `water_screen.dart` hardcodes `defaultGoalMl = 2600` while `goals_screen.dart` and the dashboard already show the real derived per-profile target — a household member can see two different daily targets on two screens of the same app, directly undercutting the "precise instrument" positioning.
- **Fix**: Have `WaterScreen` read the same `waterTargetMl` the dashboard already consumes instead of a local literal.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable audit` (functional consistency, not a visual fix).

**[P2] Bottom-nav tab "Profile" opens Settings, not the profile**
- **Why it matters**: `nourishly_shell.dart` labels the fourth tab "Profile," but `router.dart` routes `/profile` to `SettingsScreen`; the actual profile lives at `/profile/me`, reached via a row inside Settings. Directly works against recognition-rather-than-recall — the label promises one thing and delivers another.
- **Fix**: Rename the tab to "Settings" (matches the screen and its app-bar title), or route it straight to `/profile/me` and move Settings behind a gear icon.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`.

**[P2] No draft persistence for an in-progress food log entry**
- **Why it matters**: the app is explicitly designed for one-handed, easily-interrupted kitchen logging, but no `RestorationMixin`/`restorationId` exists anywhere in `app/lib`. An OS low-memory kill mid-log silently discards the entry with no recovery.
- **Fix**: Adopt `RestorationMixin` on `FoodPortionScreen`, or persist the in-progress selection to a provider that survives process death.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`.

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (Confused First-Timer)**: Taps the "Profile" tab expecting their own profile and lands on Settings' grouped list instead — a real wayfinding failure at first contact. With no meal templates built, their very first log has no guided shortcut, dropping them straight into full-text search or 7-way cuisine browsing across 441 foods. On a load failure, `goals_screen.dart` shows a raw Dart exception string with zero framing, unlike the friendlier pattern `dashboard_screen.dart` already uses.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent User)**: Component semantics are strong overall, but the proportional meal-energy bar in `daily_report_screen.dart` (a `Row` of `ColoredBox` segments) has no `Semantics` wrapper — a screen-reader user gets nothing from it (though the text rows below repeat the same information, so the loss is partial). `FocusNutrientsCard` always renders its bar in the neutral "unknown" color regardless of actual status, so a low-vision user relying partly on color loses the same green/amber/violet signal the rest of the dashboard gives. Only one raw `Semantics(...)` call exists in `app/lib` itself — every other accessibility guarantee depends on `nourishly_ui` being wired in correctly everywhere, which wasn't verified screen-by-screen.

**Casey (Distracted Mobile User)**: Water quick-add is genuinely one-tap with an Undo snackbar — the best-designed flow in the app for this persona. But logging actual food is three gated decisions on one screen with no draft persistence (P2 above); an interruption mid-log (the stove, a phone call) silently loses the entry. No "Recent" foods surface on the search screen, so Casey re-browses for yesterday's rotli and dal instead of one-tapping it back in.

## Minor Observations

- Profile setup wizard is 6 steps in code vs. the approved 5-step prototype (a dietary-preference step was added) — reasonable, but undocumented in decisions.md.
- Settings adds a "Focus nutrients" row not in prototype 13A and not among the four documented departures — again reasonable, but unrecorded.
- Error-body copy tone is inconsistent: some screens wrap raw errors in a friendly sentence, others (`goals_screen.dart`, `day_log_screen.dart`) don't.
- The quantity stepper in `FoodPortionScreen` only moves in 0.5 increments with no direct numeric entry — awkward for jumping to a specific whole-number serving count.
- `docs/design/prototype.html` itself carries 53 advisory drift findings against its own DESIGN.md scale (mostly gallery-harness chrome and illustrative shapes, not app screens) — worth a pruning pass since the file is meant to be the token-generated source of visual truth.
