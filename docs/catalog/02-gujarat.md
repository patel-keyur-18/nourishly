# Gujarat

*[Catalog index](./README.md) · ~85 items · Tier 1 daily items marked **①***

Gujarati cooking has two properties that matter for accurate tracking, and both are handled explicitly below:

- **Sweetness is structural, not occasional.** Jaggery or sugar goes into dal, kadhi, and many shaak where other cuisines use none. Omitting it understates energy and carbohydrate on a daily basis, not just on festival days.
- **Ghee and oil are used generously**, particularly in bhakhri, farsan, and the tempering (vaghar) that finishes most dishes. Fat is where portion estimates go most wrong.

## 6. Full-plate combinations worth defining as templates

Rather than logging six items, these become **meal templates** (FR-M-08) — the highest-value use of the template feature for a Gujarati household.

|---|---|

| **Gujarati thali, festive** | Above + undhiyu + puri + 1 sweet + farsan |
