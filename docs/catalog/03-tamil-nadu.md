# Tamil Nadu

*[Catalog index](./README.md) · ~80 items · Tier 1 daily items marked **①***

Three properties of Tamil cooking shape how it should be modelled:

- **The rice-and-urad batter is a shared base.** Idli, dosa, uttapam, paniyaram and adai all derive from fermented batters in different proportions. Modelling the batter as an ingredient (§1 of the common list) and each item as a recipe over it keeps them consistent and makes adding a variant trivial.
- **Oil on the griddle is the variable that matters.** A plain dosa and a ghee roast differ by 10–15 g of fat and little else. They are separate foods for that reason.
- **Accompaniments are logged as often as the main item.** Sambar, rasam, chutney and podi are eaten with almost every tiffin, so their portions need to be right — they are not a rounding error.
