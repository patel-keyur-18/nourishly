# Karnataka

*[Catalog index](./README.md) · ~70 items · Tier 1 daily items marked **①***

Karnataka needs more internal subdivision than the other two states, because the regional cuisines differ genuinely:

- **Old Mysore / South Karnataka** — rice-based, mildly sweet, close to Tamil cooking but distinct. Bisi bele bath, ragi mudde, chitranna.
- **North Karnataka** — jowar-based, spicier, drier. Jolada rotti, ennegayi, no sweetness in savoury dishes.
- **Coastal / Mangalorean** — coconut-heavy, seafood, neer dosa, kori gassi.

Treating these as one cuisine produces wrong portion sizes and wrong staples. Curate the sub-region your household actually cooks first, and leave the others as Tier 3.
