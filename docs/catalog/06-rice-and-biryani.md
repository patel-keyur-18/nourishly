# Rice dishes and biryani

*[Catalog index](./README.md) · ~5 items*

Biryanis and pulavs belong to no single regional file — a biryani is Hyderabadi by descent and cooked in this kitchen on a Sunday, and a tawa pulav is Mumbai street food — so they get their own. The catalog already carries `Vegetable biryani`, `Chicken biryani`, `Donne biryani`, `Vegetable pulao` and two `Ghee rice` rows in the regional files; this file adds only what was missing.

**Cuisine tagging:** this file is not one cuisine, so its rows take their cuisine from the section heading rather than the filename ([§0.8](./README.md#08-cuisine-and-course-tags)).

## 2. Pulav

| Food | Also | Serving | g | Composition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pudina pulav | mint pulao, pudina rice | 1 katori | 180 | Rice 55 g, onion 20 g, mint leaves 12 g, groundnut oil 10 g, coconut 8 g |
| Tawa pulav | mumbai tawa pulav, pav bhaji pulav | 1 plate | 250 | Rice cooked 180 g, potato 30 g, tomato 25 g, capsicum 20 g, green peas 15 g, butter 15 g |

Note the difference in basis between the two, and that it is not an inconsistency: a pudina pulav is cooked from **raw** rice absorbing water in the pot, while a tawa pulav is fried on a griddle from rice that is **already cooked**. Catalog spec [§0.6](./README.md#06-conventions) — cooked and raw are different foods, never a conversion — so each row names the one it actually starts from.
